Lore — What is Oshicat?

Oshicat is a semi-open night metropolis where cats cook, trade, and chase reputation under sakura lanterns. Alleys hum with lime-green glow. The harbor smells like soy and salt. Downtown never blinks.

The blue chef

OSHICAT is a street cat who stood up, tied a white headband, and decided sushi was destiny. Master Kuro still teaches in the market. The rest of the city watches to see if the stall makes it.

Districts

  • Old Market — rice, fish, and your first customers.
  • Harbor — fishing spots and late deliveries.
  • Downtown — glass towers, office cats, bigger tips.
  • Suburbs — quiet gardens and foraging.
  • Festival strip — neon food street energy.

How to earn

Everything funnels into coins, XP, and reputation. Reputation unlocks districts and shop tiers. Coins buy upgrades and ingredients.

Core loop

  1. Buy supplies at stalls (E).
  2. Open your shop, take orders, cook on the timing mini-game.
  3. Serve before patience runs out — better grades = better tips.
  4. Optional: deliveries, fishing, foraging, quests.
  5. End of day: summary, sleep, upgrade, repeat.
